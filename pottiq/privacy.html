POTTIQ

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026 · Last updated: July 3, 2026

Pottiq ("we", "our", "us") is a smart plant care reminder app developed by Ümit Can Erim. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Data stored locally on your device (standard mode):

  • Plant profiles (name, type, pot material, soil type, watering schedule)
  • Plant photos (stored on-device)
  • Watering logs and health records
  • App settings and subscription status

Data synced to our servers (Caregiver Mode only):

If you enable the Caregiver feature, the following data is securely synced to Supabase-hosted servers so your caregiver can view and act on your plants:

  • Plant profiles assigned to the caregiver
  • Watering activity logs (who watered, when)
  • Invite codes (time-limited, auto-expire after 24 hours)
  • An anonymous session identifier for the caregiver (no name, email, or phone required)

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 by Supabase. You can revoke caregiver access at any time, which immediately removes the caregiver's ability to see your plants.

Pottiq does not collect your location or financial information.

2. Third-Party Services

Supabase (Caregiver Mode only): When Caregiver Mode is active, plant and activity data is synced via Supabase (supabase.com). Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 certified. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (free users only): Ads are served via Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out in device settings.

Apple / Google Play: Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not store payment data.

3. Notifications

Pottiq uses local notifications for watering reminders. In Caregiver Mode, push notifications may be routed through Expo's notification service. Disable in device settings anytime.

4. Analytics

We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not track app usage.

5. Data Security

All server communications are encrypted with TLS 1.3. Server-side data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Access is controlled by Row Level Security policies — caregivers can only see the specific plants they are assigned to.

6. Children's Privacy

Pottiq is not directed at children under 13.

7. Data Retention

Local data is deleted by uninstalling Pottiq. Server-side data (Caregiver Mode) can be deleted by contacting us at uc.erim@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance.
