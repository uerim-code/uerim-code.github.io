Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026 · Last updated: July 3, 2026

Pottiq ("we", "our", "us") is a smart plant care reminder app developed by Ümit Can Erim. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Data stored locally on your device (never sent to our servers):

  • Plant profiles (name, type, pot material, soil type, watering schedule)
  • Plant photos (stored on-device)
  • Watering logs and health records
  • App settings and subscription status

Pottiq does not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (free users only): Ads are served via Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple / Google Play: Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not store payment data.

3. Notifications

Pottiq uses local notifications for watering reminders. No data is transmitted externally. Disable in device settings anytime.

4. Analytics

We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not track app usage.

5. Children's Privacy

Pottiq is not directed at children under 13.

6. Data Retention

All data is stored locally. Delete by uninstalling Pottiq.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance.
