FURABLY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 3, 2026 · Last updated: July 3, 2026

Furably ("we", "our", "us") is a smart pet care tracking app developed by Ümit Can Erim. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Data stored locally on your device (standard mode):

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed, birth date, weight, photos)
  • Health logs (vet visits, medications, vaccinations, symptoms)
  • Litter box / bathroom logs and behavioral notes
  • Daily check-in records and feeding schedules
  • Heat/estrus cycle data and reproductive tracking
  • Stray colony member records (if using colony management mode)
  • App settings and subscription status

Data synced to our servers (Caregiver Mode only):

If you enable the Caregiver feature, the following data is securely synced to Supabase-hosted servers so your caregiver can view and act on your pets' care:

  • Pet profiles assigned to the caregiver (name, species, breed — not health records)
  • Care activity logs (feeding, medication, litter — who performed the action and when)
  • Invite codes (time-limited, auto-expire after 24 hours)
  • An anonymous session identifier for the caregiver (no name, email, or phone required)

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 by Supabase. You can revoke caregiver access at any time.

Furably does not collect your location or financial information.

2. Third-Party Services

Supabase (Caregiver Mode only): When Caregiver Mode is active, pet and activity data is synced via Supabase (supabase.com). Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 certified. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (free users only): Ads are served via Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in device settings.

Apple / Google Play: Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not store payment data.

3. Notifications

Furably uses local notifications for vet appointment reminders, feeding schedules, and medication alerts. In Caregiver Mode, push notifications may route through Expo's notification service. Disable in device settings anytime.

4. Pet Health Data

Detailed health and behavioral data (symptoms, lab results, reproductive cycles) stays on your device and is never synced to servers. We treat this data with the same sensitivity as personal health data. Only basic care activity logs are synced in Caregiver Mode.

5. Data Security

All server communications are encrypted with TLS 1.3. Server-side data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Row Level Security policies ensure caregivers can only access the specific pets they are assigned to.

6. Analytics

We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not track how you use the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Furably is not directed at children under 13.

8. Data Retention

Local data is deleted by uninstalling Furably. Server-side data (Caregiver Mode) can be deleted by contacting us at uc.erim@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance.
